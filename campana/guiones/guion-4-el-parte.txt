PROBLEMA REAL: las quejas se repiten y el equipo nunca se entera. El dueño lee las
reseñas en su teléfono, responde con educación en Google, y la cocina sigue haciendo
exactamente lo mismo que provocó la queja. La respuesta pública es cosmética: le
contesta al cliente que ya se fue molesto y no le dice nada a quien causó el motivo.

🔴 POR QUÉ ESTE ÁNGULO Y NO OTRO: toda herramienta de reputación usa la IA para
redactar la respuesta pública. Notoria además mira hacia ADENTRO — y eso es lo que
la convierte de vigilante en herramienta de gestión. Es la diferencia que ningún
competidor está contando, así que es la que más conviene mostrar.

⚠️ EL LÍMITE: los números los pone el código, la IA solo los redacta. Si el parte
dice «seis de diez» tienen que ser seis de verdad — lo lee gente que estuvo ahí.
Por eso la tarjeta da una cifra concreta y no una impresión.

3. PROMPT DE VIDEO

A 10-second vertical 9:16 video. Cinematic observational cinematography, like a feature
film, not an advertisement.

CAMERA: 50mm lens at T2.0, shallow depth of field. One single very slow dolly-in past
her shoulder toward the kitchen pass-through across the whole ten seconds, smooth and
continuous. The focus stays on the kitchen; she remains a soft dark shape in the
foreground the entire time. No zoom, no pan, no shake.

SCENE: The restaurant from the first frame, unchanged throughout. The walls, the
counter and the pass-through window stay fixed. No object changes into another object.
The kitchen beyond the pass-through keeps the same layout for the whole shot.

SUBJECTS: The woman in the foreground and two cooks in the kitchen. Nobody speaks at
any point. All lips stay closed, all jaws still. Nobody looks at the camera.

ACTION:

       slightly. In the kitchen behind her, the two cooks keep working, plating and
       stirring, completely unaware.
4-7s   She lowers the phone and lifts her head to watch them through the pass-through.

8-10s  The light falls off smoothly and evenly until the frame settles into a flat dark

The phone screen is never legible: blown out, pure bright glare throughout.

LIGHT: The kitchen is the brightest thing in frame — warm tungsten and the glow of the
stove, with steam catching the light. The dining room around her is cooler and about
two stops darker, so she reads as a silhouette against what she is watching.
Desaturated olive and grey grade, gentle film contrast, natural 35mm grain.

AUDIO: ambient only — kitchen sounds through the pass-through, a pan, an extractor fan,
plates, distant street traffic, room tone. No speech of any kind, no narration, no
